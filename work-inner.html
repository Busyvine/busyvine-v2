Fintech

A modern payments
platform for everyday
businesses.

We partnered with Hubtel to design and build a faster, more intuitive merchant experience, helping businesses accept payments, track transactions and grow.

Visit website
Client Hubtel
Industry Fintech / Payments
Services Product Design, UI/UX,
Development, Product Strategy
Year 2024 – 2025

01. The challenge

Many Ghanaian businesses, especially SMEs, found it difficult to accept and manage digital payments. Existing tools were either too complex, unreliable or not designed for their everyday needs.

02. Our approach

We took a human-centred approach, working closely with merchants to understand their workflows, pain points and goals. We then designed and built a solution that is simple, fast and reliable — from onboarding to daily use.

User research with real merchants
Simple, intuitive interface
Scalable and secure architecture

03. The solution

A modern, easy-to-use merchant platform that lets businesses accept payments, track transactions in real time and stay in control of their operations — across web and mobile.

04. The results

3x increase in
active merchants
50% faster onboarding
99.9% platform uptime
High merchant
satisfaction

“Busyvine understood our business and designed a solution that truly works for our merchants. The result is a faster, simpler and more reliable experience.”

Alex Bram CEO, Hubtel
