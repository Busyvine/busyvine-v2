From idea to impact: what it really takes to build digital products in Ghana

Lessons from the frontlines of building useful digital products for real businesses.

Nana Owusu-Marfo

16 Sep 2026  ·  8 min read

Building digital products in Ghana is both exciting and challenging. Over the years, we've worked with startups, growing businesses and established organisations to turn ideas into products that solve real problems. This article shares practical lessons from that journey — what works, what doesn't, and what it really takes to create lasting impact.

1. Start with a real problem

The best products aren't built because they sound interesting, but because they solve a real, painful problem. We've seen how a clear understanding of the user's needs leads to better decisions, faster adoption and stronger business outcomes.

“The closer you get to the problem, the better you can design the solution.”

Nana Owusu-Marfo Head of Marketing, Busyvine

2. Design for the local context

Ghana and the wider African market have unique realities — from infrastructure and payment systems to user behaviour and trust. Designing with these realities in mind is critical. A product that works in London or New York may need significant adaptation to succeed here.

Digital products have the power to unlock new opportunities across Ghana and Africa.

3. Build, test and learn quickly

Perfection is the enemy of progress. We've learned to build lean, test with real users, gather feedback and iterate quickly. This helps us reduce risk, save costs and create products that truly meet user needs.

4. Focus on long-term value

It's easy to chase short-term wins, but the most successful products are built for the long term. That means thinking about scalability, maintainability and how the product can continue to create value for users and the business years down the line.

Final thoughts

Building digital products in Ghana is not always easy, but it is deeply rewarding. With the right problem, the right approach and a commitment to real user needs, we can create solutions that make life easier, help businesses grow and contribute to a more useful tomorrow.
