Privacy policy

Your privacy
matters.

We're committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about how we collect, use and keep it safe.

Last updated:

1.

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Busyvine collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information when you visit our website or interact with us.

2.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide to us, such as your name, email address, phone number and company details when you fill out a form, contact us or subscribe to our updates. We also collect certain information automatically when you visit our website, such as your IP address, browser type and pages visited.

3.

How we use your information

We use your information to respond to your enquiries, provide our services, improve our website, send relevant updates (with your consent) and comply with legal obligations.

4.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience, understand how our site is used and enable certain features. You can manage your cookie preferences in your browser settings.

5.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with trusted service providers who help us operate our website and business, and where required by law.

6.

Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

7.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or request the deletion of your personal information. To make a request, please contact us using the details below.

8.

Third-party links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to read their policies.

9.

Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.

10.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at hello@busyvine.com .
